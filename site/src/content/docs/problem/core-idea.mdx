<div class="principle">
	<strong>Compile both sides down to the warehouse.</strong> Every field and KPI, on both sides,
	becomes a <em>resolved definition</em>: the Redshift columns it reads, the join path that reaches
	them, the filters applied before aggregation, the aggregation and its level, and the formula on
	top. Two resolved definitions can be compared no matter how either tool organised its model.
</div>

<Mermaid
	code={`flowchart TB

## Who decides

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Tools: exact answers" icon="setting">
		Parsing, lineage, canonical proofs, join and fan-out analysis, the rule sweep. Repeatable, and
		independent of the agent.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Agent: judgment" icon="star">
		Plans the investigation, pairs objects, reasons about meaning, groups root causes, asks for data,
		explains.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Data: the tie-break" icon="random">
		When definitions can't settle it, both sides run against Redshift and the numbers decide.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
