description: Short answers to the questions people ask about the Inspector.
---

### Why not just compare the numbers of both dashboards?

Because today's data hides differences. An INNER join where ThoughtSpot used LEFT returns the same numbers until
the first row without a match arrives (scenario S06). And when numbers do differ, comparing them says *that*
they differ, not *why*. The Inspector compares the definitions first and uses data to settle what the
definitions can't, and to measure the impact.

### Why not just compare the definitions?

Some questions have no answer in the definitions: whether a key is unique, whether two different-looking formulas
return the same numbers. For those, the data decides.

### Will it work on the client's models and warehouse, not just the synthetic data?

The rules and data checks contain no names from the synthetic data; they work on concepts every model has
(columns, aggregates, joins, keys, filters). The SQL is written in Redshift dialect, the client's warehouse. The
real risk is the parsers meeting constructs the synthetic exports don't have; those end UNVERIFIED, never as
false passes. See [The rules](/how-it-works/rules/#where-they-can-still-fall-short).

### What happens when the AI is wrong?

It can't pass anything without proof, can't overturn what the data settled, and can't choose the verdict.
A wrong AI produces a weaker or less helpful report (more UNVERIFIED, worse explanations), not a wrong READY.
See [The AI and the ledger](/how-it-works/agent-and-ledger/).

### What does UNVERIFIED mean for me?

The Inspector could not prove the item either way, and says what would settle it (for example a KPI
comparison, or Strategy's report SQL). An unverified blocker makes the package NEEDS_REVIEW: a person decides.

### What is a "latent" failure?

A proven difference in the definitions that doesn't change any number on today's data. It still fails, because
the client needs numbers right from day 1, and tomorrow's data may differ.

### Ossie YAML lost the join types. What then?

What a format can't carry is listed in the IR. Checks that depend on it go to the data. If the data can't
decide either (S18: 749 orders without a customer, join type unknown), the finding stays UNVERIFIED and the
report asks for Strategy's report SQL.

### Does the developer's prompt change the result?

It adds context: what to focus on, which differences are intentional. Intentional differences are listed as
stated by the developer and unproven. It can't turn a number-changing difference into a pass.

### How long does a run take, and what does it cost?

`rules` and `rules+data` take seconds and use no model. A full agent run on S04 with Qwen3 32B on one RunPod
GPU took about 8 minutes and about 210,000 tokens before the latest changes; starting the AI from the data
results should make it shorter. On Bedrock at the client, cost depends on the model chosen.

### Where can I see what a run did?

In its trace in Langfuse, and in its run folder. See [Seeing a run](/how-it-works/seeing-a-run/).
